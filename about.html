I I'm Jay, a 2D & 3D artist originally from Portland, Oregon. I am currently located in Dublin, Ireland for an MSc in Interactive Digital Media @ Trinity College Dublin. I have a research interest in horror video games & their portrayal of sexuality, as well as their political attitudes toward death.

I have been involved in various projects & aspects of the game-making process throughout my time in undergrad, with my most recent work being Disillusion, which can be found on Steam. I'm excited to learn more about design & communication, and hopefully continue to pursue my academic career.
